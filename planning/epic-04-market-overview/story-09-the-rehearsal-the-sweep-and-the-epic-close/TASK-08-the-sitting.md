## Objective

Acceptance criterion 2: **a person has watched this screen during a live
session**, with a dated row per visible story and the `What was wrong` column
filled honestly.

**Epic 3 learned that an instrumented row does not satisfy the word _watched_,
and that the epic stayed open for nine stories because of it.**
`LIVE-REHEARSAL.md` had dated rows for four stories and not one was watched by
a human — every row said so on its face — and the owner's call was that it did
not satisfy the criterion. The owner then took the sitting.

**Seven rows are owed and they are all one session.** Five are written and
`OPEN` — Stories 4.2, 4.3, 4.4, 4.5 and 4.8. Story 4.6's is the ninth row in
the ledger and is about a reader's **hands** rather than their eyes. Task
4.7.10's is the 390 row, on a real phone, and carries its own eight-step
protocol.

**Three measurements expire with the session** and are Task 4.9.1's instrument:
each `bars` frame's symbol **list**, the `feed`-frame re-count over 75 s, and
the two-tape source note from a deployed store.

## What the user can see when this lands

**Nothing new on screen.** The ledger stops saying `OPEN`, which is the only
thing in this repository about somebody **looking** rather than about a check
passing.

## Work

- **The owner takes the sitting.** This task cannot be executed by an agent and
  must not be recorded as if it were — note 5 beneath the ledger states what a
  row of each kind may claim, and a headless watch cannot claim any of these.
- **Take Story 4.5's row first.** Ten tickers and ten percentages is the **only
  genuinely external check this epic has ever had available** — checkable
  against any public market screen in fifteen seconds. Every row above it checks
  the product against itself or against a single number.
- **Run the instrument for the whole session**, because the frame composition is
  a recording rather than a judgement and it is the item with no second chance.
- **Fill `What was wrong` honestly, including when the answer is nothing**, and
  say for each row which of the two things was watched where the row names two.
  The proxy strip's is explicit: four marks in **one** frame is a synchronised
  wave, four across several is a stagger the data genuinely has — **a rehearsal
  that cannot say which cannot answer anything.**
- **Two reversal triggers are live in this sitting** and both are conditions a
  watcher reports:
  - `The mark multiplied by five hundred.dc.html` shipped its synchrony risk
    **accepted rather than disproved**, under _the first rehearsal in which a
    reader describes the minute tick as a **flash** rather than as a **pulse**_,
    or _the first surface where the burst stops being once a minute_. The proxy
    strip is four cells on one horizontal line and does not have the density
    that carried the 518-row table.
  - Breadth's headline caption drops its direction at
    `advancing === declining`, so the **label itself changes** as the net
    crosses zero — the trigger is **the caption seen changing more than once in
    one sitting**.
- **One reading is adversarial and does not need the market to move**: pull the
  backend's feed mid-session and watch whether a **frozen count** is noticed by
  somebody who was not told to look for it. Nothing on the breadth region says
  how old a count is, by decision (Task 4.4.6), and the only surface that says
  the feed stopped is the status bar at the foot of the viewport.
- **Never ship replayed or synthetic data to the deployed site, at any hour.**
  The sitting observes the deployed product.
