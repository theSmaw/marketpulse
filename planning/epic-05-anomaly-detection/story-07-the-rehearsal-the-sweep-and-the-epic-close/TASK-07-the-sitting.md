## Objective

**This is the only task in this story a person can do, and it is the one the
epic's exit criterion turns on.** `LIVE-REHEARSAL.md` carries rows 5.1, 5.2,
5.3, 5.4, 5.5 and 5.6 — the whole of Epic 5's visible output — with `—` in every
date cell, plus Epic 4's **waived** 390 row whose `Owner:` is this epic.

A row is added by the story it names, not retrospectively by the epic close: a
row written from memory three weeks later is a row about a memory.

## What the user can see when this lands

**Nothing changes on screen.** What changes is that somebody has looked.

## Work

- **Freeze merges from 21:30.** THE-SITTING.md's Step 0: a deploy inside the
  session costs ~553 s and destroys the evidence for whatever is being watched.
  Tasks 5.7.2–5.7.6 land before the bell.
- **Follow `THE-SITTING-5.md`** (Task 5.7.1's), in its order — expiry first, and
  row **5.6** first of all, because it is the only row a sitting can clear on an
  ordinary laptop.
- **Rows 5.1, 5.2, 5.4 and 5.6** are cleared from the deployed product in one
  chair. **Row 5.3** needs a public five-minute chart and a calculator, because
  its inputs are 60 sessions of a table no browser reads — the only external
  check that will ever exist.
- **Row 5.5 is a SPLIT verdict, decided at Gate 1.** Its **refusals** are the
  shipped product and are watchable today — and the refusal a deployed reader
  meets is `too-few-sessions` (`Too little history — 80 IEX minutes at this time
of day`) at 99.990% of buckets, **not** `no-same-tape-baseline`, which is the
  permanent state of CI and every laptop and reaches no reader of the deployment.
  Its **figure** is **waived**, with its date-bound condition written on the
  waiver: 4 drawable pairs in 39,936, inside that pair's own five-minute window.
  A conforming waiver needs `WAIVED YYYY-MM-DD`, the literal `Owner:`, and ≥120
  characters of account — a bare `WAIVED` fails the build.
- **`What was wrong` is the column that earns this file.** Fill it honestly,
  including "nothing moved for four minutes", which is an ordinary observation on
  a feed with 65.1% median per-symbol minute coverage. A rehearsal with nothing
  in that column for six stories running is a rehearsal nobody did.
- **Epic 4's row 4.7** — the 390 phone sitting this epic inherited. The invariant
  will **not** force it: a waiver passes and Epic 5's arm only reads `5.x` rows.
  Watch it, re-waive it with a new dated owner, or record it as inherited and
  unmet — but take the decision rather than letting the gate's silence take it.
- **Raise `rowFloor` to 6** in the same change (Task 5.7.6 owns the line; confirm
  it landed before the rows are filled, or the anchor protects one of six).
